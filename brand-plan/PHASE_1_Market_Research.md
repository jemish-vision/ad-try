## 4. Unit economics: the numbers that decide the plan

**Fee and tax facts (verify each in Seller Central before you set prices):**
- **Referral fee: 0%** for apparel priced up to ₹1,000, effective 16 March 2026 per Amazon.in's announcement [W]. Above ₹1,000, the fee is reduced but not zero.
- **Closing fee:** about ₹20–30 at ₹500–₹1,000 [W, third-party; sources conflict].
- **Easy Ship weight handling for parcels up to 500g:** about ₹29 local, ₹43 regional, ₹63 national [W, third-party]. Amazon fees carry 18% GST, which you can claim back as input tax credit.
- **GST on garments:** **5%** for garments up to ₹2,500 per piece since 22 September 2025 [W, PIB]. Your ₹699 price includes ₹33 of GST that belongs to the government.

**Results per kept unit, at placeholder product costs** (₹260 co-ord, ₹270 cargo, ₹210 nightwear [A]):

| Scenario | Returns + RTO | Ad spend % of sales | Men's co-ord @ ₹699 | Cargo @ ₹699 | Nightwear @ ₹599 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Conservative | 35% | 25% | **–₹60** (loss) | –₹71 | –₹63 |
| Realistic | 25% | 18% | **+₹81** (12.1%) | +₹70 (10.6%) | +₹61 (10.7%) |
| Optimistic | 15% | 12% | **+₹177** (26.5%) | +₹167 (25.0%) | +₹146 (25.6%) |

**What this means:**
1. **Returns and ad spend decide whether you make money; the price you pay the supplier matters less.** At 35% returns and 25% ad spend, every product loses money even at a reasonable cost. Size charts, prepaid-order nudges and tight ad control matter more than haggling ₹10 off a supplier.
2. **Your negotiation ceiling.** In the realistic scenario, landed cost (product plus freight to you) must be **at most about ₹241 for a ₹699 product**, or **about ₹186 for a ₹599 product**, to earn a 15% contribution margin. Take these numbers into supplier talks.
3. **Price at ₹699–₹849, not ₹499.** Fixed per-order costs (₹75 in fees and shipping, ₹20 in packaging, return shipping) crush cheap products. Stay **at or below ₹999** to keep the 0% referral fee.
4. Contribution profit is not net profit. Rent, salary, the accountant, software and photography come out of contribution. The workbook keeps them separate.

## 5. Capital required (budget not given, so three scenarios)

| | Lean test | Standard | Growth |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Setup | 2 products × 2 designs × 30 units | 3 × 3 × 50 | 3 × 4 × 100 |
| Inventory (at placeholder cost) | ₹30,000 | ₹1,12,500 | ₹2,88,000 |
| Samples, labels/packaging, photography | ₹26,000 | ₹53,250 | ₹91,400 |
| Trademark + CA/registration | ₹9,500 | ₹14,500 | ₹24,000 |
| Ads for the first 60 days | ₹30,000 | ₹60,000 | ₹1,20,000 |
| Working-capital buffer | ₹25,000 | ₹50,000 | ₹1,00,000 |
| **Total** | **≈ ₹1.2 lakh** | **≈ ₹2.9 lakh** | **≈ ₹6.2 lakh** |

The buffer is there because Amazon pays out in cycles, refunds go to customers before you get the returned stock back, and ads are charged before sales settle. Sales don't become cash on the day they happen.

The **Lean test** works if a supplier accepts mixed-size small batches (30 units per design ≈ 3/7/9/7/4 across S–XXL). Many factories won't, which is one reason the Standard scenario is the realistic minimum.

## 6. Roadmap with spending gates (no money moves until the previous gate is passed)

| Phase | What happens | Gate: must be done before the next phase |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Market research | **This document** | You confirm your budget, the products, and that you're allowed to use the data |
| 2. Legal setup | Choose a business structure (sole proprietorship is likely; compared in Phase 2), PAN, current account, GST, Udyam | GSTIN issued (Amazon needs it to sell taxable apparel) |
| 3. Brand | Brainstorm 30 names; IP India search (Class 25, plus Class 35 later); domain and social handles; file TM-A (₹4,500 per class for an individual/MSME e-filing [W]) | **Trademark application number received.** This is the earliest point to print labels. |
| 4. Sourcing | 10+ verified suppliers, enquiry templates, quotes, **paid samples** | A sample passes the quality checklist and the quoted landed cost is at or below the ceiling in §4 |
| 5. Product development and QC | Tech pack, size spec, labels, packaging; inspect before paying the balance | Bulk lot passes inspection (AQL-based) |
| 6. Inventory plan | Size curve, quantities, reorder points | Purchase order placed within budget |
| 7. Amazon account | Seller Central with your GSTIN; apply for GTIN exemption or buy GS1 barcodes; Brand Registry (a pending application number may qualify; Amazon decides) | Account verified; listings live |
| 8. Listings | Titles, bullets, size charts, image plan | Listing quality check done |
| 9. Unit economics | Real fees from Amazon's fee preview replace the estimates above | Every SKU has a contribution margin of at least 15% in the realistic scenario |
| 10–13. Launch, operations, returns, finance | 30-day ad plan, daily and weekly metrics, cash-flow tracking | First profitable month |
| 14. Growth | Reorder winners, add women's co-ords, then Flipkart/Myntra, then your own website | — |

**Typical timing:** GST registration takes about 1–3 weeks. A trademark application number comes the same day you file online. Sampling takes 2–4 weeks. Bulk production takes 3–5 weeks. A realistic first sale is **8–12 weeks** from today.
